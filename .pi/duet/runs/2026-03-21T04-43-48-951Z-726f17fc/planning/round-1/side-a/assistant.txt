## Plan Summary

After thorough codebase exploration, here's my analysis and plan for the 5 open Phase 7 items:

### Key Findings from Exploration

1. **7.5 (Cross-symptom correlation)**: `correlateAcrossSymptoms()` is fully implemented with 60+ unit tests AND wired into the orchestrator. What's missing: **integration test** proving the full pipeline produces meta-findings end-to-end.

2. **7.7 (Confidence calibration)**: The benchmark script already has per-band accuracy tracking and monotonicity checks. What's missing: **unit-level calibration tests** encoding monotonicity as a regression gate.

3. **7.8 (Five dedup layers)**: Already refactored from 5 overlapping layers to `pass1ComponentMerge` + `pass2TitleDedup` + symptom suppression. What's missing: **contract tests** between passes and documentation of the dedup architecture.

4. **7.10 (Node investigation)**: `investigateNodeConditions` (NotReady, MemoryPressure, DiskPressure, PIDPressure) and `seedsFromNodes` already exist with tests. What's missing: **node capacity/allocatable analysis**, **taint investigation**, and **correlation between node pressure and OOMKilled pods**.

5. **7.11 (Declarative templates)**: `HYPOTHESIS_TEMPLATE_REGISTRY` exists with `evidenceRules` per hypothesis — but **the rules are dead code**. The `Hypothesis` type lacks the `evidenceRules` field, so rules are generated by `generateHypothesesFromTemplate()` but immediately discarded. The evidence collector has procedural functions for 7/11 categories; `oom-kill`, `generic-crash`, `bad-db-endpoint`, and `missing-service` fall through to a generic "manual investigation required" fallback.
